# Survey on Reinforcement Learning for LLM Reasoning

## TL;DR
- Reinforcement learning (RL) approaches, particularly Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO) and Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF), play a crucial role in enhancing the reasoning capabilities of large language models (LLMs) [1][2].
- Core models and architectures combine various RL techniques, such as GRPO and hierarchical frameworks, to facilitate complex decision-making processes [3].
- Training techniques focusing on effective reward modeling significantly stabilize the training of LLMs and improve reasoning accuracy [4].
- Applications of RL in LLMs are broadening with promising advancements in areas like federated learning and memory-efficient reasoning strategies [5].
- Ongoing challenges include scalability issues, effective reward modeling, and addressing biases in RL processes, pointing toward future research directions [6].

## Background
Reinforcement Learning (RL) has transformed the capabilities of artificial intelligence, enabling models to learn optimal behaviors through interaction with their environments. This survey focuses on its application to large language models (LLMs), examining both foundational theories and practical implementations that enhance reasoning abilities. The historical context traces the evolution of RL in AI, emphasizing algorithms such as PPO and RLHF as pivotal innovations in the field [1].

## Foundational Theories
The foundational theories of reinforcement learning related to LLM reasoning include key concepts that underpin the designs of LLMs. Historical frameworks were essential in establishing the theoretical grounding for the integration of RL techniques. Algorithms, such as Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO), resemble iterative processes that optimize model performance through feedback and adaptation. These foundational aspects are crucial for understanding how RL enhances reasoning capabilities [1].

## Core Models and Architectures
The architectures of LLMs now increasingly incorporate RL techniques, significantly improving their ability to reason through complex tasks. Models like GRPO and hierarchical frameworks (HiPER) exemplify this trend, supporting effective decision-making over extended horizons. These models utilize reinforcement learning to bring about adaptations that reflect real-world complexities and nuanced reasoning [2][3].

## Training Techniques
Effective training techniques play a fundamental role in applying RL to LLMs, notably through reward management strategies that help enhance reasoning processes. Utilizing conditional reward models and structured rubrics in training frameworks helps to refine the models' ability for credit assignment during sequential reasoning tasks. The development of these training techniques illustrates a progressive stride toward enhancing training stability and interpretation of reasoning in LLMs [4].

## Applications
Recent developments in applications of reinforcement learning further highlight its potential in enhancing LLM reasoning. Implementations range from memory-efficient decision frameworks to adaptive reasoning strategies exhibited in federated learning environments. The integration of verifiable rewards expands usability in various contexts, facilitating improved performance and reliability [5].

## Trends and Open Problems
Despite significant progress, challenges remain in the application of RL to LLMs. Key issues include resilience at scale, complexities in effective reward modeling, and the mitigation of biases within RL processes. Future research is poised to address these obstacles, exploring adaptive learning environments and innovative solutions to enhance understanding and decision-making in RL frameworks [6].
